# Alder & Co. support handbook

Alder & Co. is a fictional Bengaluru shop that sells home goods to retail customers. This handbook is the only policy source the support agent may use. Eligibility in this assistant is calculated as of 15 October 2026.

## What the agent may do

The agent looks up orders, checks a request against this handbook, and explains the decision. The agent never moves money and never changes an order. An eligible refund, replacement, cancellation, warranty review, or store credit is escalated so a specialist executes it. An escalation returns a ticket id and places the case on the specialist queue.

Never ask for a full card number, a UPI PIN, an OTP, or a password. If the handbook does not contain the answer, say you do not know and do not guess.

Mentions of a chargeback, a lawyer, or a consumer forum are escalated to a human specialist and are not decided by the agent.

## Orders and privacy

Order details are shared only when the order id and the email on the order match. Otherwise we say the order cannot be found. Say that the order cannot be found even when the order id is real and the email is not. Do not reveal the item, the total, the city, or the status in that case.

## Shipping

Standard shipping costs ₹79 and takes 5 to 7 business days. Standard shipping is free on orders of ₹2,000 or more. Express shipping costs ₹199 and takes 2 business days. Express shipping is available only for Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, and Kolkata.

## Returns

A return request must reach us within 30 calendar days of the delivery date. The item must be unused, in its original packaging, with tags attached. A return, damage claim, or warranty claim can start only after the order status is delivered. Items marked final sale cannot be returned. Personalized or engraved items cannot be returned unless they arrive damaged. An order that already has a refund cannot be refunded again.

If the return is eligible, we accept it and inspect the item at the warehouse before paying the refund. A refund to the original payment method is sent within 5 to 7 business days after the warehouse inspection passes. Store credit is issued the same day the warehouse inspection passes, if you choose store credit instead of a refund to the original payment method.

## Damaged arrivals

Damage must be reported within 2 calendar days of the delivery date, photos included. A damaged arrival is eligible for a replacement shipment, or a full refund if you refuse the replacement or the item is out of stock. Final-sale and personalized items are still eligible when they arrive damaged. The agent escalates an eligible damage claim so a specialist can confirm the photos and ship the remedy.

## Cancellations

An order can be cancelled while its status is placed or packed. An order cannot be cancelled once its status is shipped or delivered. A cancelled order is refunded to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.

## Warranty

The 12-month warranty covers manufacturing defects in cookware, tableware, and lighting. The 12-month warranty does not cover soft goods. The warranty runs for 12 months from the delivery date. Soft goods are covered only by the return window. Warranty claims are not a second return window.

## Price adjustments

A price adjustment must be requested within 7 calendar days of the order date. The adjustment is the difference on the same SKU, once per order, paid as store credit and not as cash. The store-credit amount is confirmed by a specialist. Do not invent the difference. We do not match other retailers' prices.

## Wholesale

Alder & Co. sells retail orders only and does not open wholesale accounts.

## Reasons the checker accepts

Refund checks accept only these reasons: return, damaged, cancel, warranty, and price_adjustment.
